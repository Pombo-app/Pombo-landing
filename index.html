Questions

Short answers.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

Mostly no. The app handles it for you. Some actions need a little crypto: POL to create a channel or your inbox, or the token a paid or gated channel asks for.

What does Pombo cost?

The apps are free and Pombo takes nothing from subscriptions. Network fees go to the Polygon network, not to us.

Who can read my messages?

Open channels are public. Protected, Closed, Gated and Paid channels are encrypted for their members, and direct messages for the two people in them. Encryption happens on your device.

Can Pombo censor me?

No. Your account is a key on your device and your channels are registered to your address, so there is nothing for us to suspend or take away. Pombo curates the Explore list in its own apps, but the protocol is open to any client, and a channel can keep its history on several storage providers at once.

What if Pombo shuts down?

Your channels are registered to your address, not to an app, and the protocol and both apps are open source. Anyone can build on them.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The web app installs to your home screen from your browser, and there is an Android app. Both are on the same network, so you can mix them.
